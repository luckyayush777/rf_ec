Faults are hard to tell apart without the debug overlay

2026-10-02 · Diagnosis

Without the debug repair status overlay telling me which part is broken, it is currently impossible to tell which part is broken. On a real GPU, though, I will mostly have one or two things to fix, not all of them, so it should be fine. Something to think about.

Current in-game tells (for reference)

  • Dust: visible on the surfaces; on the test board the fan runs faster and louder, VRAM heats up and the monitor shows memory-error artifacts.
  • Dried paste: the monitor's core temperature climbs and the clock throttles without artifacts, while the heatsink reads comparatively cool on the thermal camera.
  • Dry fan bearing: grinds while the fan spins, on the test board or when spun with the air blower.

Every new card currently starts with all three faults, so these tells overlap: dust also heats the core, which blurs the paste signature.
